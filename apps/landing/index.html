Vouch

Dating for verified college students.

Everyone on Vouch has signed in with their college email. Meet students from your campus, match when you both like each other, and chat when you are ready.

Open Vouch

Invite-only beta at JIIT Noida. Android and iOS apps are in testing.

How it works

  1. Verify

    Sign in with your college email and a one-time code. You must be 18 or older.

  2. Meet

    See profiles from your campus. Like or pass; a match needs both of you.

  3. Chat

    Only matches can message each other. You can unmatch at any time.

Safety first

  • Real students only

    College email verification, and every photo is checked before it is shown.

  • Block and report

    Blocking hides you from each other everywhere. Reports reach our moderators.

  • Your data

    We never store your email address in plain text. You can download your data or delete your account from the app.

Delete your account

In the app or on the web, open Settings and choose Delete my account. Your profile, photos, messages you sent and likes are erased within 30 days.

On the web: open Settings (sign in with your college email first). If you cannot sign in, write to {{GRIEVANCE_EMAIL}} from your college email.
